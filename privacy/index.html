Privacy policy for the Hearth household application and its information website.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who operates Hearth

Hearth is a private household application provided under the 90 Labs name. The household operator controls its self-hosted installation and account access. Questions or requests about your information can be sent to poptartxtreme@gmail.com.

This policy applies to Hearth. It does not describe unrelated 90 Labs services.

Information Hearth accesses

  • Google identity: your verified email address and Google account identifier, used to recognize an approved household member. Hearth stores a household display name and account preferences. It does not receive your Google password.
  • Calendar list: calendar identifiers, names, colors and access permissions, used to let you choose calendars. Unselected calendar details appear only in your authenticated picker and are not stored as household calendar records.
  • Selected calendar events: event information needed to display, synchronize and edit your chosen calendars. The local cache includes identifiers, titles, dates and times, version information, household attribution and all-day or recurring-event status.
  • Household content: lists, meal plans, chore records, preferences and photos that members supply or maintain in Hearth. Uploaded photos become resized display copies without source EXIF or GPS metadata; original photo files are not retained by the app. Hearth does not request access to Google Photos.
  • Connection data: Google authorization credentials, session and display-pairing records, and technical service logs needed to operate and secure the installation. Logs may contain network metadata such as IP addresses, timestamps and response status. OAuth authorization codes and tokens are excluded from access logs.

Google permissions and how they are used

Hearth requests Google sign-in and email permissions, read access to your calendar list, and permission to view and edit Calendar events. Google’s event permission is account-wide. Within Hearth, no calendar is selected automatically and event contents are requested only from calendars you explicitly select and save.

Hearth uses this information to authenticate members, display shared plans, synchronize selected calendars, and save supported event creations, edits and deletions to Google when you request them. Changes require your account’s write permission for the selected calendar. Current all-day and recurring events are displayed read-only in Hearth.

Hearth does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Hearth’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your information

Selected calendar events and household content are shared with approved household members and authorized paired displays. Pairing is intended for a trusted household screen: anyone who can physically see that screen may see its displayed content, and permitted checkoffs do not require a PIN.

A configured household voice integration may read permitted calendar and chore information. Its capability does not allow changes or access to uploaded photos. Hearth currently does not send Google user data to a cloud AI service.

Google processes authentication and Calendar API requests under Google’s privacy policy. The operator and people expressly authorized to administer the household installation may access stored data when providing support or maintaining the service. This information website does not connect to the household database.

Storage and safeguards

Household data is stored on the operator’s self-hosted system. Google refresh credentials are encrypted at rest, with their key kept separately from the application database. The application uses HTTPS, protected session cookies and household access controls. These measures do not make data visible on a physically accessible paired screen private from people nearby.

The public information pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or household sign-in. Their hosting service may process ordinary request metadata to deliver and protect the pages. The household app uses necessary session and display cookies and may save a non-sensitive slideshow preference in your browser.

Your controls, retention and deletion

Use Calendar connections in Hearth to change your selected calendars. When no household member still shares a calendar, Hearth removes its imported events and synchronization records. Google remains the source of the original events; unselecting or disconnecting a calendar does not delete those originals.

Disconnect Google Calendar removes your stored Google connection credentials and selections, clears data no remaining member shares, and attempts to revoke the Google grant. If revocation cannot be confirmed, you can remove access through your Google Account connections. Disconnecting the provider does not delete your household identity or automatically revoke every paired display.

Contact the operator to request removal of your household access or stored account data. Household identity, contribution history and other household records can remain after a provider disconnect; the operator must review a broader deletion request separately. Use the app’s controls to remove lists, meal entries and photos you are authorized to manage, and unpair displays that should no longer have access.

Hearth maintains a limited event cache around the current date. If synchronization fails, older saved information can remain until synchronization succeeds or its calendar is removed. Other records remain until deleted or removed by the operator. Deleting active records does not rewrite existing backups; ask the operator about any retained backup copies when requesting deletion.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if Hearth’s data practices change. Its date identifies the current version. New data uses or additional Google permissions require an updated explanation and, where needed, your consent.

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact poptartxtreme@gmail.com.
