Your account, your choice

Connect only what you want to share.

  1. Sign in with Google

    Hearth uses your verified Google identity to check household membership. Google handles your password.

  2. Choose your calendars

    Calendar names appear in your private selection screen. No calendar is selected automatically; event contents are imported only after you select and save a calendar.

  3. Plan with your household

    Selected events become visible to household members and authorized displays. Supported edits save back to Google Calendar. You can change your selections or disconnect later.

Google grants Calendar event permission across your account. Hearth applies an additional boundary in the app: it imports and edits events only in calendars you explicitly select. Read the privacy policy for details about storage, sharing and deletion.
